FAQ

Is it free?

Yes, the bot is free. There are hourly and daily limits (check /limits). Optionally, get VIP with Telegram Stars or for free by inviting friends (/ref).

Can I download just the audio (MP3) from YouTube?

Yes, for free: send a YouTube link and tap “🎵 Audio (MP3)”. MP3 from SoundCloud is free too; MP3 from videos on other sites is a VIP feature.

Do I need to register?

No. All you need is Telegram — open the bot and send a link.

Which sites are supported?

YouTube (including Shorts), TikTok, VK Video & Clips, Pinterest, X (Twitter) and SoundCloud. Instagram and Reddit are coming soon.

Can I use it in a group?

Yes. Add @DownGrabBot to your chat with the “Add to group” button — it responds to links and commands and ignores regular messages.

Something doesn't work — where do I ask?

In the support chat or message the owner directly: @kartkino.
